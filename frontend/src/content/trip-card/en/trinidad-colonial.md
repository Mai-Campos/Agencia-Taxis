Explore one of the best-preserved colonial cities in the Caribbean. We walk through Trinidad’s cobblestone streets, visit the Main Square and its traditional museums, and later explore the historic Manaca Iznaga Tower. The tour ends in the Valley of the Sugar Mills, full of rural landscapes and old haciendas.
