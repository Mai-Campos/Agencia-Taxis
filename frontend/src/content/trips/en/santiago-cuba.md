This tour takes you through Cuba’s most Caribbean-influenced city. We visit the imposing Morro Castle guarding the bay, then head to Céspedes Park and the Diego Velázquez House, the oldest colonial home in Cuba. We finish with a cultural walk along Garzón Avenue, where music and tradition fill every corner.
