We head to one of Cuba’s most beautiful valleys: Viñales. We visit the Mural of Prehistory painted over a mogote, then ride a boat inside the Indian Cave. The tour finishes at Los Jazmines Viewpoint, offering a spectacular panorama of the valley. Ideal for nature lovers.
