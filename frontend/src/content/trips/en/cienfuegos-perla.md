This tour highlights the elegance of Cienfuegos, a city with unique French influence. Visit José Martí Park and the historic Terry Theater, stroll through Punta Gorda with its coastal mansions, and explore the stunning Palacio de Valle. Perfect for lovers of architecture and history.
